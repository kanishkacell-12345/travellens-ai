
# TravelLens AI — Prototype Demo Script

Suggested duration: 2 minutes

## 1. Introduction — 0:00 to 0:15

"Hello, we are Team She Coders, and our project is TravelLens AI — Global Smart Heritage Explorer.

Our aim is to make landmark discovery and cultural exploration easier by bringing heritage knowledge, local stories, and travel assistance into one platform."

## 2. Problem — 0:15 to 0:35

"Travellers often need to switch between different sources to find reliable historical information, local cultural knowledge, nearby attractions, and safety information. Language and connectivity barriers can make this harder."

## 3. Website Overview — 0:35 to 0:55

"Here is our TravelLens AI interface. The Discover section provides a searchable collection of sample landmarks. Users can search by name, country, region, or category."

## 4. Landmark Details — 0:55 to 1:15

"When we open a landmark, we can view its location, historical information, local-story notes, and a travel tip. The sample records can be expanded using trusted sources."

## 5. Image Preview and Community Stories — 1:15 to 1:35

"The photo feature currently previews an uploaded image. Our next development step is connecting a trained recognition model. The community form lets users preview a local story before a future review and verification workflow."

## 6. Future Scope — 1:35 to 1:50

"Our roadmap includes CNN or TensorFlow Lite recognition, OpenCV preprocessing, trusted heritage APIs, smart routes, nearby services, OCR, currency conversion, and multilingual assistance."

## 7. Closing — 1:50 to 2:00

"TravelLens AI aims to connect trusted heritage knowledge with verified local voices and smarter travel assistance. Thank you."

## Recording Notes

- Show the actual website and demonstrate working interactions.
- Do not describe image preview as AI recognition.
- Do not describe sample records as verified unless they have been checked.
- Explain which features are implemented and which are planned.
